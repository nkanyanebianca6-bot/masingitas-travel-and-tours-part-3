About Masingitas Travel and Tours

A South African travel agency creating memorable journeys and connecting travellers with exceptional destinations since 2008.

Our Background and History

Masingitas Travel and Tours was founded in 2008 by entrepreneur Khanyisa Masingita Ngobeni, who wanted to share South Africa's scenic beauty with the world. After a strategic pause, the business relaunched in 2018 with new digital systems, wider commercial partnerships and a clear focus on affordable, high-quality travel management for local and international clients.

Today we are a VAT-registered agency with professional accreditations from aviation and tourism bodies. Our goal is to make tourism accessible, convenient and memorable while supporting growth in Southern Africa's economy.

Our Mission, Vision and Core Values

Our Mission

To provide convenient, affordable and customised travel solutions for individuals, families, organisations and schools, so that every journey, local or international, runs smoothly and safely.

Our Vision

To become a trusted digital travel management company recognised across Southern Africa for excellent customer care, reliable service and innovative online booking.

Our Core Values

  • Integrity: honest, transparent and accountable business.
  • Customer focus: itineraries built around your preferences and budget.
  • Reliability: verified accommodation, airline and transport partners.
  • Excellence: continuous improvement at every stage of travel.

Who We Serve

  • Families and leisure groups: holiday planning, resort bookings and group getaways.
  • Corporate and government travellers: business travel, executive shuttles and conference venues.
  • Couples and honeymooners: romantic weekend escapes and luxury lodge stays.
  • Schools and academic groups: safe educational tours, museum visits and heritage trips.
  • International visitors: inbound travel, safaris and guided city tours across South Africa.

Accreditations and Compliance

  • IATA accredited: certified by the International Air Transport Association to issue domestic and international air tickets.
  • ASATA member: a member of the Association of South African Travel Agents, following its professional code of conduct.
  • VAT registered: operating in line with South African commercial and tax regulations.
